## ABOUT

I am Yaser Daher, founder of **ELECTRON VISION**, an independent technology initiative focused on designing and developing advanced computing systems and software technologies.

My work centers on systems engineering, operating system architecture, low-level development, cybersecurity, cryptography, and next-generation computing infrastructure.

I believe meaningful technological progress comes from understanding systems at their foundations and engineering solutions that can stand independently.

## FOUNDER — ELECTRON VISION

**ELECTRON VISION (ELVI)** is a technology initiative dedicated to developing an independent ecosystem of software, computing, security, and infrastructure technologies.

Our long-term vision is to build reliable, secure, efficient, and innovative technologies that contribute to the future of computing.

### Core Technology Areas
